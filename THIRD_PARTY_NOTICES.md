# Third-Party Components

## stb_image

The repository includes `stb_image` version 2.30 by Sean Barrett and contributors.
The complete upstream dual-license text (MIT or public domain) is preserved at the
end of `Solar System/stb_image.h`.

## GLAD and Khronos platform declarations

`third_party/glad` contains an OpenGL 3.3 loader generated by GLAD 0.1.36. The
generation metadata is preserved at the top of `glad.h` and `glad.c`.
`khrplatform.h` retains its original Khronos copyright and permission notice.

## GLFW and GLM

The build downloads GLFW 3.4 and GLM 1.0.1 from their upstream Git repositories
through CMake `FetchContent`. These sources are not redistributed in this
repository; their upstream license terms apply to the downloaded dependencies.

## Texture assets

The original repository did not include source or license information for the Sun,
planet, Moon, or skybox image files. Unused skybox images and fictional moon
textures were removed during cleanup. The retained planet and Moon images are used
as illustrative educational assets. Their provenance and redistribution terms
should be established before the images are reused or distributed independently.
